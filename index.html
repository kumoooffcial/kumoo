Founder - Red Mark Studios

Founded 2024 | Team: 5 Members | Downloads: 10,000+ | Education: BBA

What started as a passion for gaming during my school days has evolved into Red Mark Studios. With a vision to create something better, I founded the studio with a dedicated team of 5 talented individuals.

Our first game, "The Last Floor," achieved 10,000+ downloads in 1.5 months. We're building Red Mark Studios as a creative force in the gaming industry, where every project pushes boundaries and delivers exceptional experiences.

About

I'm kumoo, a writer and founder passionate about storytelling and creating experiences that matter. I've always believed that stories are how we make sense of the world.

Through short fiction, I explore the emotional truths that statistics and facts can't capture. I'm driven by the desire to create work that resonates deeply with readers.

Current Focus: Short fiction exploring family dynamics and personal transformation
