Founder Profile

Building the future of gaming, one game at a time

What started as a passion for gaming during my school days has evolved into Red Mark Studios. With a BBA background and a vision to create something better, I founded the studio in 2024 with a dedicated team of 5 talented individuals.

Our first game, "The Last Floor," achieved 10,000+ downloads in just 1.5 months—a testament to our commitment to quality and innovation. But this is just the beginning. We're building Red Mark Studios as a creative force in the gaming industry, where every project pushes boundaries and delivers exceptional experiences.

Our mission is clear: create games that matter, that challenge players, and that leave a lasting impression.

About the Writer

I've always believed that stories are how we make sense of the world. Whether through the compressed power of poetry or the narrative arc of short fiction, I write to explore the emotional truths that statistics and facts can't capture.

My work examines the intersections of identity, memory, and human connection—the quiet moments that change us, often without our knowing.

Current Focus
