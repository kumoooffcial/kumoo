Script Writing Samples

A few short-form scripts written for gaming and challenge-style video content — paced for narration and on-screen action.

Rising Ground — Minecraft Challenge
Gaming / Challenge
WAIT—WHY IS THE GROUND RISING?! Every second, the blocks underneath me rise higher and higher... And then suddenly, THE ENTIRE GROUND SHOOTS UP! I had no idea what was happening, so I mined underneath the raised blocks... Nothing. Just AIR. And underneath that? DEATH. Luckily, I was right next to a water source, so I jumped straight into it just as the ground started rising again. I survived, but now I needed to figure out how to use this mod to my advantage. So I found a tree, crafted a crafting table, and decided to hunt for diamonds. Because whenever the ground rises, it also brings whatever is underneath it up with me. I kept exploring until I found a village with a smithing house. Inside, I found some iron ingots, so I crafted an iron bucket and continued exploring. Then I found an iron golem and took it down for even more iron. I built myself about three blocks up... And right on cue, THE GROUND ROSE AGAIN! I just sighed... "Thank god I crafted that bucket." Because if this thing launches me into the sky, I'm going to need a water bucket clutch. So if I actually survive the clutch, you better subscribe! And then... A cave appeared. I rushed inside... And finally... DIAMONDS! After all that chaos, I finally found what I came for. This mod is completely insane. But I'm not stopping here. I'm finding diamonds no matter what this mod throws at me. LET'S GOOOOOO!
Cushion Script
Gaming / Tutorial
If you place a cushion on a shelf, you can sit on it, get off, and even walk across it normally. But if you walk over it from one specific spot, you'll fall straight through into a hidden base. You can make that spot even more precise by placing bamboo around it. But you can also do the opposite. Place a cushion on a fence, and it looks completely normal. You can walk across it, and it elevates you just like a fence. However, once you sit on it and try to get off, you'll pass straight through it. So you can place these randomly in the middle of a cushion line to create a hidden entrance... Or put one at the very end to turn it into a trap.
